Meetings, decisions, and action items arrive two ways: automatically, from an hourly pass over calendar, email, and Fellow, and manually, by asking Claude to log a pasted or attached transcript - needed for Teams meetings, since Teams transcript access isn't automated. Either way this page updates live for every viewer the moment something is written. A digest of overdue and soon-due items is emailed each morning. Status pills cycle open → in progress → blocked → done on click.
